[scene-09] BACKLINK LIMITATION
Slide title: THE BACKLINK CAVEAT

Here's the honest caveat worth knowing before you pay. Morningscore's backlink database is powered by Moz — and Moz's index is noticeably smaller and slower to update than Ahrefs or Semrush. If link-building research is your primary workflow, you will feel that gap. The tool is strong on everything else; backlinks are where it punches below its weight class.
